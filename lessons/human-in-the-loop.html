Agentic AI

Human-in-the-Loop

Learning path: Agentic AIIntermediate6 min readLast updated: October 2026

2-Minute Summary: Human-in-the-loop (HITL) means people participate at intentional points in an automated or AI-assisted process. A person may validate uncertain information, approve a consequential action, or handle an exception. Good checkpoints provide the context and authority needed to make a decision without turning every routine step into a bottleneck.

What This Means

Human review is not a failure of automation. It is a design choice that balances speed, risk, and accountability. The right checkpoint helps a process continue safely when confidence, policy, or context calls for human judgment.

Simple Explanation

Think of a review gate like a traffic light. The system can proceed on routine, validated cases; pause for approval on high-impact changes; and route exceptions to someone who can resolve them. The person should see why the process paused and what options are allowed.

Real-World Example

A document workflow may extract invoice fields and compare them with expected ranges. If values agree, the process continues. If an amount is missing or unusually high, it presents the source document and extracted values to a reviewer before submission.

How It Works

Define the decision boundary, the trigger for review, the information shown to a reviewer, response options, time limits, and what happens if no one responds. Record the decision and route returned work to a safe next step.

Diagram

Automate→Human review→Resume safely

Key Takeaways

  • Place review where judgment meaningfully reduces risk or improves quality.
  • Give reviewers evidence, clear choices, and enough time to decide.
  • Plan for timeout, rejection, escalation, and an auditable outcome.

Common Mistakes

  • Requesting approval without showing what is being approved.
  • Sending every low-risk case to a person, creating an avoidable queue.
  • Failing to define ownership or what happens when approval is delayed.

Practical Exercise

Choose an automated decision. Write down what makes a case uncertain or high-impact, what evidence a reviewer needs, what actions are available, and what the workflow does after approval, rejection, or timeout.
